# 0014 Era themes retuned to Adam's era breakdown

**Date:** 2026-11-26. **Decided by:** Adam shared an era-by-era visual breakdown (lighting, colour, camera for
1959-64, 1985-89, 2002-03 and 2019-20) and approved retuning the themes ("yes", 13:34 UTC). Updates decision 0010.
The palettes and textures were chosen in the thread, shown to him as screenshots before merging.

## Decision

- **1959 Original:** unchanged palette (black and white, grain, grey photographs), plus the shadow of venetian
  blinds across the top-right corner and a low fog, both behind the text (German Expressionism and noir).
- **1985 Glow** (was 1985 Neon): warm, earthy amber and brown with electric blue links and violet for the
  unofficial shelf, a soft haze and a gentle bloom on titles (diffused light, warm domestic tones, electric
  blues and purples for the supernatural). Heading face changes to Fraunces.
- **2002 Steel:** drained greys, cold blue links, a hard fluorescent green-white accent and light from above;
  photographs lose most of their colour (drained flesh tones, overcast skies, harsh fluorescents, cold moonlight).
- **2019 Gloss** (was 2019 Crimson): glossy teal and orange, a wide clean face (Michroma, moved from 1985), page
  titles in plain white over a teal-to-orange rule like a stark title card (glossy, saturated, symmetrical).
- Theme keys stay `neon` and `crimson`, so a visitor's saved choice still works; only the names in the menu change.
- Same rules as 0010: palette, type and texture only, no logos or title designs, every colour pair passes the
  contrast check, and motion stops under reduced motion.
