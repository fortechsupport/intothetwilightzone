# 0001 Design direction: Midnight and Paper

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **Decided by:** Claude (redesign thread), on Adam's request for "a professionally designed, logically
  structured website that fans will bookmark". Adam reviews this.

## Decision

A dark default theme (Midnight: near-black blue, warm off-white text, brass accent) and a light theme (Paper:
cream, ink, deep amber), from design/tokens.json with a contrast test. Type: DM Serif Display for headings,
Jost (a geometric sans in the Futura tradition) for text and interface, Courier Prime for narration and
teleplay excerpts. Original artwork only: an abstract door and vortex, no imagery from the show.

## Why

- The 1959 series is black and white, typographic and mid-century. Brass on midnight reads as that era
  without copying its title cards.
- Geometric sans faces were the type of late-1950s television and print; a high-contrast serif carries the
  "living history" voice. All three faces are free (SIL OFL) and self-hosted, so no visitor data goes to a
  font service.
- A light theme matters for long reading and for visitors who set their system to light.
- The show's own images and title cards are copyrighted; original art avoids that.

## Set aside

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| Keep the system fonts | Looked generic; Adam asked for a professional design |
| A spiral or the show's title-card imagery | Copyrighted; also a cliché of fan sites |
| Eight themes, as Seymour has | Suits an app, not a reading site; two are enough |
| Google Fonts | Sends every visitor's address to a third party |
