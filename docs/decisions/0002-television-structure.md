# 0002: Television structure

**Date:** 2026-11-21 (08:56 and 09:15 UTC) · **Decided by:** Adam · **Plan:** docs/plans/research-television.md

## Decision

1. **Television is one category** in Watch, holding four subsections:
   - The Twilight Zone (official series and revivals)
   - Rod Serling's other series, including Night Gallery
   - similar anthology series
   - Homages

   Episodes nest under their series. Every series covered has its own page.
2. **Episode pages only for the official series and revivals** (1959, 1985, 2002, 2019). Night Gallery is one page
   with a dynamic way to explore its episodes. Its episode pages are retired. Every other non-TZ series uses the same
   explorer.
3. **Similar anthology series are tagged** however each tag makes sense.
4. **Homages show in two places:**
   - on the page of the show they are in (for example The Simpsons)
   - on the TZ page they relate to (for example "Nightmare at 20,000 Feet")

   One not tied to a particular episode shows on its show's page and on the TZ series page. Homages are filterable
   by type, series and more.
5. **TV films go in Movies**, which is worked after Television.
   - Pilots and unmade projects related to TV series or episodes (but not TV films) are included in Television.
   - A documentary made by a series, such as American Masters' film on Serling, belongs on that series' page, and
     is also listed in Movies when it is feature-length.
   - Broadcast history, home video and streaming are included for every series (official, revival and other),
     with links to home video products and to where each series can be streamed now.
6. **A play made for any TV series goes in Television**, and also in Movies if it is feature-length.
7. **Networks and narrators** (and by extension showrunners, production companies, writers, directors and composers)
   show in Television as links. Their pages stay in Organizations and People.

## Why

Television is the root of the franchise. Episode pages are kept for the official series, where visitors expect
them. A single, explorable page keeps Rod Serling's other work and related series complete without hundreds of
thin pages.

## Set aside

- **Keeping Night Gallery's 43 episode pages** (Claude's first recommendation). Adam wants Night Gallery as one
  page, like the other non-TZ series.
- **Homage entries listed only under their show.** Adam wants each homage also on the TZ page it relates to.
- **TV films and TV documentaries moved into Television.** They stay in Movies. A series' own documentary shows on
  that series' page too.
