# 0017 Discover comes first, Read lists seven formats, and every category is one table

**Date:** 2026-11-28. **Decided by:** Adam, in the project thread "Reorganize the menu into seven groups" (14:19 UTC,
corrected at 14:29 UTC: "Discover, View, Listen, and Read should have 1 table per category, not group").
Details marked *(thread)* were decided while building; docs/plans/navigation-2026-11-28.md logs them.
Supersedes the Meet and Read lists of decision 0015.

## Decision

- The menu: **Discover, View, Listen, Read, Play, Collect, Legacy**. Discover is Meet renamed (Organizations,
  People, Places, Events); /meet/ redirects to /discover/.
- **Read** lists exactly seven items, A to Z: Articles, Books, Comics, Essays, Magazines, Scripts, Short stories.
  Short stories replaces "The stories behind the episodes" (/reading/source-stories/ redirects) and adds Serling's own
  short stories. Its topic collections stay on the Read page, not in the menu.
- **Websites** leave Read and become a kind of link in Legacy, "Websites and fan sites" (/homages/websites/;
  /reading/websites/ redirects). *(thread: Adam's "all the kinds of links"; no record dropped.)*
- **One sortable, filterable, searchable table per category:** each category page in Discover, View and Listen,
  each Read format page, and the Play, Collect and Legacy pages. One row per record, with a reason on every row; a
  filter for every column; sort by any column; A to Z by default; filters kept in the address.
- Play's table covers every game, the nods in other games, fan-made games and the games on this site. Collect's
  covers every shelf a fan can own, with a Physical or digital column taken from what each record says. Legacy's has
  Kind of link, Category and Evidence columns. *(thread)*

## Why

Adam asked for it. One table per category lets a visitor see, search and sort everything in a category at once,
which the shelf previews could not; the shelf pages still show Official and Unofficial apart.
