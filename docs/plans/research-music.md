## 3. On every page

**A theme or score release:**
- Composer, conductor and performers, label and catalogue number, release year and format.
- Track list where a source gives it, with each cue linked to its episode.
- Where to listen, from the label's or the streaming service's own page.
- Liner-note writers and producers.

**A song:**
- Artist, songwriters, album, year and label.
- How it connects: "about the show", "names the show", "uses the phrase", "covers the theme" or "samples the theme".
- Where in the song, in our own words. At most the few words that name the show are quoted (Q2).
- The artist's or label's own page and the lyric source we read, both linked.

**An episode page (Television):** a "Music" line naming the composer of an original score, or "stock music from the
CBS library" where a source says so.

## 4. Media and documents that attach (never categories)

- **Album covers:** linked from the label or a catalogue. They are also listed in Artwork, credited to the designer.
- **Liner notes and reviews:** reviews of soundtrack releases attach to the release, with the critic and
  publication named. Liner-note essays attach to the release.
- **Composer interviews** attach to the composer's People page.
- **Official audio:** official uploads by the label or artist are linked, never hosted.

## 6. Sources and fetch budget

- **Theme and scores:** the labels' own catalogue pages (Varèse Sarabande, Silva Screen, Film Score Monthly, La-La
  Land, Intrada), Discogs release pages for credits and catalogue numbers, Wikipedia, and Reba Wissner's
  writing on the show's music.
- **Samples and covers:** WhoSampled and SecondHandSongs.
- **Lyrics:** read on a lyrics site (the method that worked in 2026-11: letras.mus.br), linked, never copied.
- **Episode music:** published episode guides and the score releases' track lists.
- **Serling on record:** Discogs, as a lead that needs a second source.

**Budget:** about 80 fetches for the 63 unconfirmed songs, and about 60 for the themes, scores and episode music.
That is two rounds of under an hour each at the shared 100 an hour.

---

## 7. Blessing-ready

- **Accuracy a rights holder would trust:**
  - Composer and label credits come from the release itself or the label.
  - A sample is "confirmed" only with the artist's credits or WhoSampled. Otherwise it is "Noted by fans".
- **Respect for their property:**
  - No audio is hosted. Lyrics are never copied; at most the few words that name the show are quoted.
  - Album covers are linked, never copied.
- **Honoring the people:** every composer, songwriter, performer, conductor and liner-note writer is named. Constant
  and Herrmann are credited for the theme everywhere it appears.
- **Credit where credit is due:** each source's author and publication is named, and WhoSampled and SecondHandSongs
  contributors are credited as researchers.
- **Sending fans to official sources:** where to listen links go to the label's or the service's own page.
- **No money:** no affiliate codes on any store link, no ads, no sponsorships.
