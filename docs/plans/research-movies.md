decade and type filter. The shelves are:

Patterns, The Rack, Requiem for a Heavyweight, Seven Days in May, Planet of the Apes, The Man, The Doomsday Flight, the 1969 Night Gallery pilot and In Search of Ancient Astronauts all go here, and so do later films of his scripts (The Salamander, 1981; A Storm in Summer, 2000). | Official (his work) |

## 3. On every film page

1. **Header:** type, year, the Official or Unofficial label, and one line on the connection.
2. **Facts:**
   - release date and runtime
   - studio and distributor (Organizations links)
   - director, writers, and the story it's based on with its author
   - cast with their characters (People links)
   - composer
3. **Why it's here:** the TZ episode or Serling work it connects to, how, how sure we are, and the source. This is
   the same block homages already use.
4. **About:** a short history in our own words, with sourced dates covering development, production and
   release. For Twilight Zone: The Movie it includes the 1982 accident, written factually and naming the three
   people who died with respect.
5. **Where to watch** and **home video**, using the same components and non-commercial link rules as Television.
6. **Media**, all link-only:
   - the official trailer, if a studio or rights-holder channel hosts it
   - the poster, credited to its artist
   - reviews by author and publication
   - interviews and featurettes
7. **Themes** inherited from the TZ episode it points to, as now.

**The Movies landing page:**
- the six shelves
- the Official and Unofficial rows
- filters: shelf, type, decade, TZ episode, confidence
- text search, with A to Z (ignoring The, A and An) or chronological order

## 4. Media and documents that attach (never categories)

- Trailers, posters, lobby cards and press kits attach to their film. Each is credited to its maker and rights
  holder, and linked, never copied.
- Featurettes and Blu-ray extras attach to the film or series they cover. Examples: "The Wandering Man's Burden"
  (on The Loner) and the 1983 film's DVD commentary, if one exists.
- Reviews are by named critics, such as Roger Ebert on the 1983 film, if found.
- Box office figures and contemporary press, each with its own source, go in the About text.

## 7. Blessing-ready

- **Accuracy a rights holder would trust:**
  - Credits come from AFI, the on-screen credits or the studio. Wikipedia alone is not enough for an official
    film.
  - "Inspired by" is never stated as fact without a source. A link resting only on fan sources says "Noted by
    fans".
- **Respect for their property:**
  - No stills, clips or posters are hosted.
  - Trailers are linked only from the studio's or rights holder's own channel.
  - The site never uses the official logo or title design.
- **Honoring the people:**
  - Full credit for the authors of adapted stories, as the 1983 film's record already does for Richard Matheson,
    Jerome Bixby and George Clayton Johnson.
  - Serling's film work is presented as a body of work.
  - The 1982 accident is written factually and respectfully.
- **Credit where credit is due:**
  - every poster artist, critic and interviewer by name
  - every source by author and publication
  - researchers and fans who supply a source, credited as in Television
- **Sending fans to official sources:** where to watch and home video link only to licensed services and
  publishers, with no affiliate codes.
- **No money:** no ads, no affiliate links, no sponsorships.

  sheet.
