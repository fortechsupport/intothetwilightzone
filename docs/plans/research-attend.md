## 1. Where Attend stands today

Events has 97 placements on five shelves; Lectures 53 on five. Problems found on 24 November 2026:
- **"Other events" is 39 records of Serling's life** (born 1924, the war, marriage, his death) plus the 1982
  accident and a Grateful Dead concert. The life events already belong to History > Rod Serling's life; here they
  repeat, and 33 of them have no decision, so the site hides them everywhere.
- **"Screenings and marathons" mixes in broadcast history** (the 1963 hour-long season, the last first-run episode,
  the 1985 revival's scheduling, syndication and the "lost five"): those are History.
- **Every event is marked unofficial**, even CBS's own 2019 PaleyFest and WonderCon panels, the Fathom 60th
  anniversary screenings and Disney's Tower of Terror anniversaries. Fan screenings sit beside network marathons
  as official.
- **"Rod Serling's lectures and speeches" lists talks *about* Serling** by others (the Everson Museum, SUNY Oswego,
  Rosendale Theatre, Reba Wissner, Tim Madigan, Mark Boulton), because a title naming him counted as his.
- **Serling's own teaching** (his Antioch seminar, Ithaca seminars and screenings, the Ithaca "Writing for
  Television" videos) sits in *In the classroom* with school lesson plans.
- An interview (KPFK, 1970) is a shelf of its own instead of media on Serling's page.
- 46 records in Events, History and Lectures have no decision and are hidden.

| Shelf | Groups |
|---|---|

*Readings and panels* folds into *Talks about the show* (it had one keynote).

**Where the misfiled records go:** Serling's life events to History > Rod Serling's life only; broadcast milestones
to History > Production and broadcast history; the Tower of Terror's California closing to Production and broadcast
history; the USPS stamp to History > Honors; the Grateful Dead's 1985 performance of the theme to Music > Covers;
the KPFK interview to media on Rod Serling.

## 3. On every page
Date, place (linked to Places), organiser, who spoke or appeared (linked to People), what was shown, and a source.
Upcoming events say so and keep their date.

## 4. Media and documents that attach
Recordings of talks (Ithaca, KPFK, Library of Congress), programmes and press coverage attach to their event and
appear in the Media Library, link-only.

## 5. Cross-links
Venues to Places, speakers to People, organisers (Rod Serling Memorial Foundation, Ithaca College, Bundy Museum) to
Organizations, episodes screened to their pages.

## 6. Sources
Organisers' pages, local press (Press & Sun-Bulletin, WBNG), university news, archive catalogues (Ithaca College,
Pacifica Radio Archives, Library of Congress), broadcasters' schedules.

## 7. Blessing-ready
Events by the rights holders (CBS, Disney, Fathom for CBS) are labelled official; fan and foundation events are
credited to their organisers by name. Recordings are linked to the archive that holds them, never copied.

## Decided here (Adam reviews later)
- The structure above.
- **Official events:** events run by the rights holders: CBS's 2019 PaleyFest and WonderCon panels and the Series
  Mania world premiere, the Fathom 60th-anniversary screenings, Disney's Tower of Terror anniversaries, and network
  marathons (KTLA, WPIX, Syfy, H&I). Fan and college screenings are unofficial.
- **The 46 hidden Events, History and Lectures records are included** (Adam's rule of 2026-11-23); each has a source.
- A talk is Serling's only when Serling gave it (the record's creator), not when its title names him. The Ithaca
  "Writing for Television" videos are recordings of Serling teaching, so their record now names him as creator.
- The KPFK interview (1970) is media on Rod Serling's page and in the Media Library, not a shelf.
- The Grateful Dead's 1985 Berkeley show goes to Music > Covers, beside the band's recording of the theme.
- Fan and college screenings (Ithaca's 2008 marathon, Serling on Screen) and the Stage 89 fan gathering are
  unofficial; everything else keeps the site's rule (official only where the rights holders ran it).
