### Out (with its home)
- Theatrical films go to Watch > Movies, and radio to Listen. Lights Out, Suspense and Inner Sanctum Mystery are
  radio series filed as TV today. Suspense also had a TV run Serling wrote for, so it stays, labelled as both.
- Scripts go to Read > Scripts. The episode page links to them.
- Merchandise and home video discs go to Collect. The series page lists them (see D5).
- Reviews, interviews, ads and documents are content in the Media Library, shown on the episode or series they
  are about.

### Edge cases
- **Series by Twilight Zone writers** such as Earl Hamner Jr.'s The Waltons and Falcon Crest are not anthologies or
  homages. Suggestion: a small "Series by Twilight Zone writers" shelf in C, tagged "creator link".
- **Shows compared to TZ, not tied to it**: The Good Place has a sourced twist comparison. It stays in Homages,
  tagged "comparison", and is never presented as inspired.
- **Duplicates to merge:** `films/monsters` and `films/monsters-1988`; `films/duel` and `films/duel-1971`.

## 2. What each page shows

**Television landing page (/watch/television/).** An intro in our own words, then the four subsections as shelves,
A to Z (the official series in air order, because order is the point there). Below them sit the panels from D4
and a year and network filter.

**Series page** (every series in A, B and C, and whole series in D):
- Header: years, network or streamer, number of seasons and episodes, creator, showrunner, narrator or host,
  production company, and its official or unofficial label
- About: an own-words history (origin, run, cancellation, revivals) with sourced dates, which also feed the Timeline
- Episodes:
  - for A, a season-by-season list linking to every episode page
  - for B, C and D, entries on the page (anchored, with air date, writer, director, a one-line summary and a source)
- People: writers, directors, cast, composers and crew, each linking to their People page
- Characters, settings and filming locations
- Music: the theme, scores and soundtrack releases
- Where to watch now, and home video releases
- International titles
- Legacy: homages and remakes of this series
- Media: reviews, interviews, ads, press kits, archive holdings (the Serling papers, the CBS collection) and links,
  all link-only unless public domain
- Why it's here: the connection to TZ, its tier and the source

**Episode page (A only):**
- air date, season and number, production code
- writer, source story and author, director
- cast with their characters
- an own-words synopsis, with no transcript and no reproduced narration
- adaptations: the radio drama, comic, remake and 1983 film segment
- homages in other shows, its script in Read, music cues, filming locations
- sourced trivia
- reviews and media, and where to watch

## 4. Sources to mine, and the fetch budget

- Wikipedia articles on Rod Serling, on each anthology Serling wrote for (Kraft, Studio One, Playhouse 90, U.S.
  Steel Hour, Climax!, Suspense), on every series in B and C, and on Night Gallery's episode list. These fetch
  reliably from here.
- Archive finding aids already on the site: the Rod Serling Papers, the CBS collection and the Dictabelt
  collection. They name teleplays and air dates.
- Library catalogues and TV-history sites for the live-TV era, used only when the page names its own source.
- IMDb, TV Tropes and the Fandom wiki refuse automated fetches. They count only as leads, never as a source.

**Budget:** about 350 fetches, about 4 hours at the shared 100 an hour. That is three or four rounds, best run
overnight:
- Serling's teleplays: about 120
- re-sourcing the anthologies: about 40
- checking homages by show: about 150
- organizations and home video: about 40
