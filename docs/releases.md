## 2026-11-24 — Music research round 2: who scored the episodes

Episodes can now say who wrote their music:
- 27 episodes have an original score with its composer and sources, among them Herrmann's "Walking Distance", Goldsmith's "The Invaders", Van Cleave's "Perchance to Dream" and Waxman's "The Sixteen-Millimeter Shrine". The sources are Varèse Sarabande's LPs, Silva Screen's 40th Anniversary set and Bill Wrobel's rundowns of the scores held at UCLA.
- Seven episodes are marked as stock music from the CBS library. Three of them list the library composers from their cue sheets.

Four of Varèse Sarabande's original-score LPs now list their tracks. The Night Gallery themes have a second source, and the record of the pilot's theme now says its score was never released.

**Known not to work yet:**
- The music line doesn't show on episode pages until the structure step.
- About 120 original-series episodes still have no music information.
- No record by Rod Serling himself has been found.

## 2026-11-24 — Music research round 1: the unchecked songs, checked

Of the 62 songs listed as "Connection not yet confirmed", eight now say how they connect to the show, with a link to the page we read:
- Billy Creepshow's "In The Twilight Zone" and Rise From Your Grave's "The Twilight Zone" are songs about the show. Billy Creepshow's lyrics name six episodes, and those episodes now link back to the song.
- "Twilight Zone" turns out to be a cover of Golden Earring's song on Seeking Major Tom (2011), the album by William Shatner, who starred in two episodes.
- The Gubs name the show in their lyrics.
- Four songs use only the everyday phrase.

Fourteen more songs are confirmed to exist, with their albums and years. The other 38 are marked with what was searched. Six songs found along the way are added:
- the great Luke Ski's parody about the 1959 series.
- S.G. Smitty's album built from the series' own sound.
- Covert Flops' "The Midnight Sun", based on the episode.
- Ivan Campo's "And When the Sky Was Opened".
- Two songs that use only the phrase.

The Music plan (six shelves, six questions) is approved and is in docs/plans/research-music.md.

**Known not to work yet:**
- The songs still sit on the old shelves until the structure step.
- 52 songs are still unconfirmed.
- The full list of covers of the theme is still missing, because SecondHandSongs could not be read.

## 2026-11-24 — Radio: Rod Serling's radio years, and the first fan audio drama

Radio now holds Rod Serling's whole radio career as far as sources record it, each with its source. A visitor can
hear the young Serling acting in WNYC's "Toward Return to Society" (11 December 1946), streamed by WNYC from the
New York City Municipal Archives, and read about his student station at Antioch College, his scripts for Party Line,

the Chair", and Suspense's former director Elliott Lewis directed The Zero Hour. Two new kinds of radio record
appear: the 2020 WVXU radio play of Serling's "O'Toole from Moscow", narrated by his daughter Anne Serling, and
Dylan Vance's fan-made audio drama of "Escape Clause", which is marked unofficial and also listed under Fan-made.

**Known not to work yet:** These still sit on the old radio shelves under group headings; the five shelves come in

their star except six.

## 2026-11-23 — Fan votes: thumbs up and down on every record (switch on with fan accounts)

**Fans can vote on anything on the site.** Once fan accounts are switched on (docs/ACCOUNTS_SETUP.md):
- Every record page (people, characters, places, organizations, episodes, films, books, songs and the rest: 6,045
  pages) shows a **thumbs up** and a **thumbs down** with their counts, next to Favorite and Later.
- So does every single link between records (566 homages, parodies, references and adaptations), wherever it is
  listed, so fans can vote on one tie, such as a film link noted only by fans.
- A signed-in member votes once per record, and can switch or withdraw their vote. Anyone can see the counts.
- When a record's score (ups minus downs) falls below zero, Adam sees it on the Moderation page under **Votes below
  zero** and decides: **Keep it** or **Mark for removal**. Nothing is removed automatically.

- No alert emails until email sending is set up; the Moderation page is the alert until then.
- Reviews, quotes and clips in the Media Library that aren't records of their own can't be voted on yet.
- Lists don't show counts and can't be sorted by score.
