# Release notes

What a visitor can do after each change, in plain words. Newest first. Every entry ends with what is
known not to work yet.

## 2026-11-24 — More cover artists, Gold Key comic credits, essay bylines

Visitors can now:
- See who made the cover of 40 of the 60 issues of The Twilight Zone Magazine (23 more than before), among them Ralph
  Mercer, Clayton Campbell, Malcolm McNeill, Walter Velez, Carl Wesley, Richard Newton and Hajime Sorayama, each read
  from the issue's own contents page, a review of the issue or a catalogue record, and linked to the artist's page.
- See who wrote, drew, inked and lettered 18 issues of Gold Key's Twilight Zone comic (1962-1974), such as Dick Wood,
  Leo Dorfman, Frank Thorne, Reed Crandall, Alex Toth and the painted covers of George Wilson, from the Grand Comics
  Database's credits.
- Read the author and date of 27 more articles, essays and interviews, from each article's own page.
- Learn that Rod Serling wrote the foreword to Johnny Hart's B.C. Strikes Back, and when Patterns (1957) and the Night
  Gallery Reader (1987) were published.

**Known not to work yet:**
- 8 magazine covers have no credit in any source we can read.
- 76 comics, 135 essays (author) and 77 essays (date), and 34 books (date) are still being filled in, in paced rounds.

## 2026-11-24 — Magazine cover artists, more first printings, three reference sites

Visitors can now:
- See who painted or photographed the cover of 17 issues of The Twilight Zone Magazine, from Jim Warren's first issue
  (April 1981) and Magritte's "The Pleasure Principle" (May 1981) to Gahan Wilson, J. K. Potter and Gottfried Helnwein,

**Known not to work yet:**

- Comic creators are missing on 94 issues, because the Grand Comics Database cannot be read automatically.

## 2026-11-24 — The stories behind the episodes, and a tidier Read shelf

Visitors can now:
- Browse **The stories behind the episodes** (Read): a page for each published story, radio play or book an episode
  of The Twilight Zone or a Night Gallery segment adapts, from Damon Knight's "To Serve Man" to H. P. Lovecraft's
  "Pickman's Model", grouped by show. Each says who wrote it, who wrote the teleplay and, where a source we read says
  so, the magazine or book it first appeared in.
- See on every episode page the story it is **based on**, linked, and an **In print** list of the books, comics,
  magazines and essays that reprint, adapt or discuss that episode. Night Gallery segments link their stories too.
- Find every book on one shelf only: script books now sit on Scripts and teleplays alone, every book has a group, and
  Matheson's and Bradbury's novels sit under "Books by the show's writers" instead of under Night Gallery.
- Find Rod Serling's own books (his story collections, the Night Gallery books, Patterns and the Gauntlet script
  volumes) in "Rod Serling's own writing", beside his articles and talks.
- Read twenty more reviews, from the New York Times on Patterns (1955) to AudioFile on the radio dramas (2005).

**Known not to work yet:**
- Some stories have no first publication yet: each page says so. Ten episodes credited only "from a story by" someone
  have no story page, because no source names the story.
- Three AudioFile reviews now redirect to another site, so their verdicts could not be read.
