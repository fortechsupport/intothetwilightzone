## Rules used for the feed facts

- **Episode count:** the number the feed lists, dated 2026-11-24. A feed can cap how many episodes it lists
  (Strange Highways' feed lists 400, Ear of the Beholder's 300), so a higher count from an earlier source is kept,
  and the source note says what the feed lists.
- **First episode:** the feed's earliest date, unless an earlier sourced date exists (feeds drop old episodes).
  Kept earlier dates: Between Light and Shadow, Ear of the Beholder (2015 vs the feed's 2021), Strange Highways,
  The Key of Imagination, The Twilight Zone Zone, Zone-i-Sodes.
- **Status:** active when the feed has an episode from the six months before the read (on or after 2026-05-24),
  otherwise ended. "Ended" is an inference from the feed, and each source note says so.
- **Segments** (Hallowed Groundling): only episodes whose titles name the show are kept (10 of the feed's 37).
- **Single episodes of other podcasts** are never read whole (their feeds are about other things).
