- S.G. Smitty and Steven Keith, The Twilight Zone (2013): an instrumental album built only from the series' samples (core).
- Covert Flops, "The Midnight Sun" (2021, Eccentric Pop Records): the band says it is based on the episode (core), with a connection to the episode.
- Ivan Campo, "And When the Sky Was Opened" (2024): episode title, from an album called The Twilight Zone; the tie is our reading (connection marked fan-noted).
- Carnal Machinery and Echoberyl, "Twilight Zone" (2021, 2024): phrase-only lyrics (tangential, per Q1).
- Connections: Billy Creepshow to six episodes named in its lyrics, Rise From Your Grave to "The Monsters Are Due on Maple Street", Covert Flops and Ivan Campo to their episodes.

## Corrections
- `warren-haynes-twilight-zone`: the track is on William Shatner's Seeking Major Tom (Cleopatra, 2011), a cover of Golden Earring's song (George Kooymans). Artist set to "William Shatner with Warren Haynes". The 2026-11-21 pass could not find it.
- `peboop-jimdog-the-peboop-dance`: the artist and title are spelled "Peeboop" on Apple Music. The record id is unchanged so its decision key still holds.
- `freedom-fry-mr-nobody`: features A-F-R-O. `spoken-nerd-tokens`: credited to Spoken Nerd and Juan Cosby.

## Samples and covers
- 36 sample records: 32 already cite WhoSampled. `michael-jackson-threatened` now cites WhoSampled too, for two narration samples. The other three cite Wikipedia or the Twilight Zone Wiki, which meets Q3.
- No new samples were found: WhoSampled searches for the theme, the narration and Serling return only songs already recorded.
- SecondHandSongs (the covers database) refused automated reading (client error). The covers on record (The Ventures, The Manhattan Transfer, Neil Norman) stand. A full covers list needs another source.

## Methods
- Bandcamp track and album pages show lyrics and credits, and were the best source this round. Apple Music artist pages show only top songs and recent singles, so "not listed" there is weak evidence. Apple rate-limits after about 10 quick reads.
- SoundCloud, WhoSampled and SecondHandSongs refuse automated reading. Letras still falls back to a different song when a slug is wrong.
