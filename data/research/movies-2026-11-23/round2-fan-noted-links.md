# Round 2: checking the fan-noted film links (2026-11-23)

Adam, 2026-11-23 22:18 UTC: "Mentions from fans about movies having ties are okay, but should be verified."
Every fan-noted link from a feature film has now been checked at least once (2026-11-20, or earlier on
2026-11-23 against Wikipedia and critics' lists). The 26 below were read again, this time against the makers'
interviews; the rest have no maker interview left to read and keep their earlier notes. Rule kept: a critic's comparison stays "Noted by fans";
"Documented" needs a source reporting the tie as fact; "Confirmed by its makers" needs the maker's words.

- Raised to documented: Devil (Film Stories, Mark Harrison, 2024-08-09, on Shyamalan's Night Chronicles).
