# Movies round 1: Rod Serling's films checked against their own pages (2026-11-23)

Each film's English Wikipedia article (infobox and body), read 21:42 UTC, and the Rod Serling Memorial Foundation's
"Rod Serling's Complete Filmography" (Stephen Schlich, 20 July 2019, https://rodserling.com/rod-serlings-complete-filmography/).

## Foundation filmography, "Movies" section (13 entries; it gives director and stars, not Serling's credit)
Patterns (1956); The Rack (1956, lists Stewart Stern as director); Saddle the Wind (1957); Incident in an Alley (1962);
Requiem for a Heavyweight (1962); The Yellow Canary (1963); Seven Days in May (1964); Assault on a Queen (1966);
The Doomsday Flight (1966, TV); Planet of the Apes (1968); Night Gallery (1969, TV); The Man (1972);
The Time Travellers (1976, TV; the only entry made after Serling's death). No documentaries, narrations or unmade projects.
All 13 are on file (night-gallery-pilot, time-travelers-1976). Two Foundation slips, noted not copied: Stewart Stern wrote
The Rack's screenplay (Arnold Laven directed), and Saddle the Wind opened 5 March 1958.
