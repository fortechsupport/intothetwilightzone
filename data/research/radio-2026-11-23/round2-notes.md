# Radio research, round 2 (2026-11-23): shelves B to E

## WNYC, 1946: Serling as an actor
- WNYC, "Radio from 'The Twilight Zone'", Cara McCormick, 23 August 2012 (NEH Preservation Project annotations):
  https://www.wnyc.org/story/227277-radio-twilight-zone (22 min; "Audio courtesy of the NYC Municipal Archives WNYC
  Collection"). Also "Radio from The Twilight Zone", From The Archives, 6 May 2010:
  https://www.wnyc.org/story/44463-radio-from-emthe-twilight-zoneem (5 min excerpt).
- The program: "Toward Return to Society", a public-service series made with the New York City Department of
  Correction, 11 December 1946. Serling plays a boy who thinks stealing a radio is "his ticket to the future". He
  was "an unpaid intern at WNYC" in his Antioch freshman year, with newsroom and script-writing duties.

## Cincinnati
- University of Cincinnati News, "Rod Serling's early career preserved by UC professor", Angela Koenig, 6 October 2025:
  H. Michael Sanders (professor emeritus, UC Blue Ash) has collected Serling's early scripts, including around 40
  episodes of the live drama "The Storm" (WKRC-TV). Serling was a staff writer at WLW radio and secretly freelanced
  for WKRC-TV.
- The Ohio Newsroom (WVXU), Kendall Crawford, 3 October 2025: the same, plus Antioch College and his brief teaching
  there.
- WVXU, John Kiesewetter, 12 September 2019: Serling was hired to write for WLW radio and TV after graduating in 1950
  and wrote for WKRC-TV's "The Storm" in 1951-52. WVXU and the UC College-Conservatory of Music planned a radio
  production of Serling's 1955 NBC Matinee Theatre play "O'Toole from Moscow" (adapted by Kiesewetter, directed by
  Richard Hess, CCM students), to be recorded in fall 2019 and broadcast in 2020. Whether it aired is not confirmed
  by this article. A shelf C candidate (another radio adaptation of Serling's work).

## The Zero Hour (1973-74), hosted by Serling
- Wikipedia, "The Zero Hour (American radio series)": premiered in syndication 3 September 1973; picked up by the
  Mutual Broadcasting System in December; last episode 26 July 1974. Hosted by Rod Serling (no writing credit).
  Producer J. M. Kholos; director Elliott Lewis; Don Hills produced for StudioHouse. 130 episodes counting each
  part of the five-part serials (season 1: five-part stories Monday to Friday; 1974: one story per 30-minute show).
  Highbridge Audio released six of the five-part stories on cassette ("Desperate Witness", "Face of the Foe", "But I
  Wouldn't Want to Die There", "Wife of the Red-Haired Man", "The Heir Hunters", "If Two of Them Are Dead").
- Flapper Press, John C. Alsedek, "Back to His Radio Roots: Rod Serling & 'The Zero Hour'", 17 June 2019: Elliott
  Lewis directed, J. M. Kholos produced; season 2 stars included Mel Tormé, Jackie Cooper, Dick Sargent and William
  Shatner; "what the show didn't have was sponsors".
- CrimeReads, Keith Roysdon, 25 June 2026: Serling "hosted The Zero Hour but didn't write its stories"; Keith Walker
  wrote 26 episodes; calls Elliott Lewis the producer (the other two sources say director).
