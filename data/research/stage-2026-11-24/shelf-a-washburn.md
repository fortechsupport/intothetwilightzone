# Shelf A: Anne Washburn's The Twilight Zone (read 2026-11-24)

## Almeida Theatre, London, 2017-18 (world premiere)
- https://almeida.co.uk/whats-on/the-twilight-zone/ : "5 Dec 2017 — 27 Jan 2018". Director Richard Jones. Set Paul Steinberg;
  costume Nicky Gillibrand; choreographer Aletta Collins; lighting Mimi Jordan Sherin; composition and sound Sarah Angliss;
  sound Christopher Shutt; music direction Steve Bentley-Klein; casting Julia Horan CDG; illusions Richard Wiseman and Will
  Houstoun. Cast: Oliver Alvin-Wilson, Franc Ashman, Adrianna Bertola, Lizzy Connolly, Amy Griffiths, Neil Haigh, Cosmo Jarvis,
  John Marquez, Matthew Needham, Sam Swainsbury. Credit line: "Based on Stories by Rod Sterling [sic], Charles Beaumont, and
  Richard Matheson, Adapted by Anne Washburn". Also: transferred to the Ambassadors "and ran from 4th March to 1st June 2019".
- https://playbill.com/article/what-did-critics-think-of-the-twilight-zone-london-world-premiere (Adam Hetrick, 2017-12-14):
  "officially opened December 12"; critics listed: The Arts Desk (David Nice), Daily Mail (Quentin Letts), Evening Standard
  (Fiona Mountford), Financial Times (Ian Shuttleworth), The Guardian (Michael Billington), The Hollywood Reporter (Stephen
  Dalton), The Independent (Paul Taylor), The New York Times (Matt Wolf), The Stage (Tom Wicker), The Telegraph (Dominic
  Cavendish), Time Out London (Andrzej Lukowski), Variety (Matt Trueman). Linked reviews not read individually.
- https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/review/twilight-zone-theater-1067132 : Stephen Dalton. "Presented by Almeida Theatre, in
  association with Ron Fogelman." Finds it "More spoofy than spooky"; praises the design and the later sections.
- https://www.denofgeek.com/tv/the-twilight-zone-play-how-the-stories-were-selected/ (Chris Longo, 2019-03-17): the eight
  stories: And When the Sky Was Opened; Eye of the Beholder; Will the Real Martian Please Stand Up?; Little Girl Lost; The Long
  Morrow; Nightmare as a Child; Perchance to Dream; The Shelter. Ron Fogelman, producer, took "nearly three years to convince
  CBS they had the right plan in place"; CBS "is rightfully selective about who is granted licence". So the play is licensed by
  CBS (this article's account).

## Ambassadors Theatre, West End, 2019
- https://playbill.com/article/cast-set-for-west-end-bow-of-the-twilight-zone-stage-adaptation (Ryan McPhee, 2019-01-09) and
  https://broadway.com/buzz/194554/casting-set-for-west-end-transfer-of-anne-washburns-the-twilight-zone (Andy Lefkowitz,
  2019-01-09): begins 4 March 2019; director Richard Jones. Returning: Oliver Alvin-Wilson, Adrianna Bertola, Neil Haigh. New:
  Alisha Bailey, Natasha J Barnes, Nicholas Karimi, Dan Crossley, Dyfan Dwyfor, Lauren O'Neill, Matthew Steer.
- Reviews: The Arts Desk, Rachel Halliburton, 2019-03-13, 4 stars
  (https://theartsdesk.com/theatre/twilight-zone-ambassadors-theatre-review-retro-wit-our-new-space-age-0); WhatsOnStage, Alun
  Hood, 2019-03-13, 4 stars (https://www.whatsonstage.com/news/review-the-twilight-zone-ambassadors-theatre_48688).

## Citadel Theatre, Edmonton, 2025 (North American premiere)
- https://citadeltheatre.com/shows/24-25-twilight-zone/ : "May 3 - May 25, 2025"; "the highly anticipated North American
  premiere"; director Richard Jones. The page's credit line names "Keith Reddin and Anne Washburn" as adapters; no other source
  read says Reddin worked on the play, so it is recorded as the Citadel's credit only. No cast on the page.
