# Shelves B to E (read 2026-11-24)

## Who licenses what (the Rod Serling Memorial Foundation's own answers)
- https://rodserling.com/faq-items/who-controls-the-rights-to-rod-serlings-work/ : "CBS owns all Twilight Zone rights. Not the
  Serlings, not the Foundation." Requiem for a Heavyweight or Patterns: Concord Theatricals. "For almost everything else":
  Rick Berg, Code Entertainment. Night Gallery: "MCA/Universal".
- https://rodserling.com/faq-items/i-just-watched-a-most-profound-episode-entitled-night-of-the-meek-which-first-aired-in-1960-the-script-just-begs-to-be-staged-if-not-fully-then-certainly-as-a-radio-play-do-you-know-if-it-ha :
  "CBS owns the rights that you need to stage any episode; you must contact CBS." "The Serling family can grant permission
  for a reading only, but it usually must be free to the public." "To our knowledge, 'Night of the Meek' has never been staged."
- Consequence for Q2: a staging of an episode is "licensed" only where a source says CBS (or CBS/Paramount) gave permission.
  A source saying the Serling estate or family approved is recorded as that ("approved by the Serling family, per <source>"),
  not as a licence.
- The Stage landing blurb says companies "stage episodes from the published scripts"; no source read supports "published
  scripts". Rewrite in the structure step.

## Shelf C: Serling's own work on stage
- Requiem for a Heavyweight, Broadway (IBDB https://www.ibdb.com/broadway-production/requiem-for-a-heavyweight-4364): Martin
  Beck Theatre; first preview 1985-02-28, opened 1985-03-07, closed 1985-03-09; 8 previews, 3 performances. "Written by Rod
  Serling". Director Arvin Brown. Originally produced by the Long Wharf Theatre. Producers Zev Bufman, Ken Butler, Walter Barnett,
  Ivan Bloch, with Jujamcyn Theaters. Cast includes John Lithgow (Mountain McClintock), George Segal (Maish Resnick), Maria
  Tucci (Grace Miller), David Proval (Army Hakes), Dominic Chianese (Max Greeny). Lithgow: Tony nominee and Drama Desk winner 1985.
- Requiem, Shattered Globe Theatre, Victory Gardens Greenhouse, Chicago, to 2008-03-08; director Lou Contey; Sean Sullivan.
  Review: Mary Shen Barnidge, Windy City Times, 2008-01-23 (https://www.windycitytimes.com/lgbt/Theater-Requiem-for-a-Heavyweight/17314.html).
- Requiem, Crystal Theatre, Norwalk (Connecticut), to 2025-10-19; director Mark Ferguson; Gus Bottazzi produced and played
  Mountain. Review: Sherry Shameer Cohen, BroadwayWorld, 2025-10-13
  (https://www.broadwayworld.com/article/Review-REQUIEM-FOR-A-HEAVYWEIGHT-at-Crystal-Theatre-20251013).
- Requiem, RiverTree Theatre (Westword, https://www.westword.com/?p=40612747): no date or city in the page text. Held back.
- Patterns, stage adaptation by James Reach. Theatre 40, Reuben Cordova Theatre, Beverly Hills, to 2015-08-23; director Jules
  Aaron; producer David Hunt Stafford. Reviews: Steven Stanley, StageSceneLA, 2015-08-13 (https://stagescenela.com/2015/08/patterns/);
  Ed Rampell, People's World, 2015-08-14 (https://peoplesworld.org/?p=15115).
- Patterns, staged reading by Southern Tier Actors Read with the Rod Serling Video Festival, Binghamton High School Black Box
  Theatre, Oct 11 and 12 (year not on the page); director Bill Gorman (https://broomearts.org/?p=3268). Held back: no year.
- Serling's own IBDB person page failed to load; only the Requiem production credit is confirmed.

  Beholder", "Living Doll", "The Invaders" as drag comedy. TheaterMania listing only; held back.

- In the Zone with Rod Serling, Hopewell Theatre, Youngstown, 2025 (five episodes, not named); director Christopher Fidram;

- Binghamton University 2014 (the existing record): university release 2014-03-06 gives dates 7-8 and 14-16 March 2014, Watters

## Foundation and overview pages (second batch)
- Den of Geek, 2019-04-29 (https://www.denofgeek.com/tv/the-twilight-zone-on-stage-a-classic-enters-a-new-dimension/): for
  Washburn's play, CBS granted the rights ("It took some time for me to convince CBS that we were the right people to go
  with", producer Ron Fogelman), and the producers "courted the blessing of the estates of Serling, and Twilight Zone
  screenwriters Charles Beaumont and Richard Matheson". Serling's narration uses "audio from the original episodes". No
  history of earlier stagings.
- Foundation newsletter, summer 2025 (https://rodserling.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Serling_Newsletter_summer_2025_Final.pdf):
  BLAST and the Rod Serling Memorial Foundation gave a fundraising dramatic reading of "The Happy Place" (1958 pilot script)
  at the Cider Mill Playhouse, Endicott, New York, the second weekend of July 2025; director Kate Murray. A second
  reading of the script after SerlingFest 2019. Recorded on the existing Happy Place record (same script, new reading).
- Foundation fall 2025 newsletter (https://rodserling.com/fall-2025-newsletter/): read only as a summary past a cut-off, so
  not used for facts. It covers the Crystal Theatre Requiem (Oct 10-12 and 17-19, 2025) and a Rod Serling Evening at the
  Rosendale Theater (2025-10-02, a talk with clips, not a staging).
- Paley Center 2009 reading (https://rodserling.com/an-evening-at-the-paley-center, Andrew M. Polak): cast Lucie Arnaz, Fritz
  Weaver, Robert Walden, Laurence Luckinbill, Katharine Luckinbill, Jeremy Hollingworth; narrator Michael Citriniti; director
  Antony Marsellis; Serling's recorded narration for the opening and closing; Anne Serling-Sutton read from her memoir.

  production yet (Found, for later).
