# Series run facts, 2026-11-21

Fetched from each series' Wikipedia article (infobox and lead) for Television plan section 5. Applied to the
`films` records by `node scripts/apply-series-facts.mjs`. Only facts the page states were kept.

Left out on purpose:
- Alfred Hitchcock Presents: the infobox ends the run on May 10, 1965, the body on June 26, 1965, so only "1965" is kept.
- Fear Itself: 13 episodes were made but only 8 aired, so no episode count.
- Journey to the Unknown: its listed hosts introduced only four American TV films, so no host.
- Tales from the Crypt: the host is a character (the Crypt-Keeper), so no host.
- Night Gallery: the article's first date is the 1969 pilot; the series record keeps its December 16, 1970 premiere.
- Suspense: the record covers the radio and TV series, and the article is the radio one, so nothing applied.
- Are You Afraid of the Dark? (page did not load) and One Step Beyond (a disambiguation page): not applied. Research round 2.
- Series with no Wikipedia link in their record (Black Mirror, Dimension 404, Ultra Q and others): research round 2.
